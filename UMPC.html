Custom UMPC

About Work Contact

I have been fascinated with laptop technology for years, and one of my favorite form factors is the ultra-mobile personal computer (UMPC). UMPCs typically feature five- to seven-inch displays and weigh less than 2.5 pounds. I wanted to create my own seven-inch UMPC using a Raspberry Pi.

I began by selecting the components. The core of the computer consists of a Raspberry Pi and a seven-inch touchscreen display. I then moved on to designing the housing, which went through several iterations. Initially, I planned to purchase a hinge, but after 3D-printing the first prototype, I realized that the hinge needed to be integrated into the housing itself.

Another challenge was finding a keyboard PCB that fit the form factor. To solve this problem, I designed and assembled a custom 40-key keyboard using KiCad. I also programmed multiple keyboard layers so that I could type every character needed while keeping the keyboard compact.

The final steps were assembling the computer and installing its operating system. Because the Raspberry Pi has limited computing power, I chose a lightweight Arch Linux installation using the Hyprland tiling window manager.

Although I was happy with the first version, I learned that standard USB and HDMI connectors are impractical in such a small form factor. For the next revision, I plan to design additional custom PCBs and use flat-flex or other high-density connectors for the internal components.
