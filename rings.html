Aluminum Rings

About Work Contact

After recently expanding my jewelry collection, I became frustrated that even simple rings often cost more than $20. I decided to make my own using a metal lathe. I purchased aluminum round stock from McMaster-Carr and developed a repeatable machining process.

I began by cutting the stock to a manageable length and securing it in the lathe chuck. I faced the end of the stock and turned the outside diameter to the desired size. Next, I drilled through the center and used a boring bar to machine the ring's inside diameter. This allowed me to produce rings in specific sizes.

I then machined a simple design into the outer profile and used a parting tool to separate the finished ring from the remaining stock. Finally, I deburred, filed, and polished the edges to create a smooth and comfortable finish.
